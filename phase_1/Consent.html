Information Sheet

Title of the project: Effects of Echo Chambers on Opinion Polarization.

Name and contact information of the PI: Prof. Michele Starnini michele.starnini@upf.edu and Prof. Vicenç Gómez i Cerdà vicen.gomez@upf.edu, Department of Engineering, Universitat Pompeu Fabra. Roc Boronat building (Poblenou campus) Roc Boronat, 138 08018 Barcelona.

Institution: Universitat Pompeu Fabra

Funding body: This project is funded by project CNS2022-136178 financed by MCIN/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 and by the European Union Next Generation EU/PRTR.

Objectives of the project: The goal of this study is the role of echo chambers in the propagation of information and polarization in regards to climate change.

Methodology and participation:
This is the recruitment phase for a longer longitudinal experiment. Today, you will complete a short screening questionnaire (approx. 4 min).
The screening questionnaire will ask basic demographic questions, including political affiliation, and your opinion on climate change. You will be paid the stated fixed amount ($1.00) for completing today's session.

Please be aware: This is a screening study for a longer experiment. Please proceed only if you are able and willing to commit to a multi-day study. If you are eligible, you will be sent an invitation to the main experiment where:

  • You will be asked to log in daily, at a time of your convenience, for 5 additional days.
  • Each day, you will complete another round of the main experiment (approx. 2 min). Each round consists of reading a feed of news headlines and deciding which ones you are willing to share with other participants.
  • On the final day, you will also complete a post-experiment survey (approx. 4 min) asking for your opinion on the subject matter again, as well as feedback on your experience.
  • You will be paid a base rate for every additional round you participate in of $0.50/round, except the last round which pays $1.50.
  • You will receive an additional bonus ($5.00) if you complete at least 4 of the 5 additional daily rounds.
  • If you complete all 5 additional rounds (perfect attendance), you will be entered into a lottery for an additional $100.00 prize.

Inclusion criteria: Participants must be residing in the U.S and be over 18 years of age.

Voluntary participation: Your participation in this study is on a voluntary basis and you may withdraw from the study at any time without having to justify your decision.

Contact information: If you have doubts, contact Aleix Nicolás Olivé at aleix.nicolas@upf.edu or any of the principal investigators. If you have complaints, or questions about this study or about your rights as a research participant, you may contact UPF’s Institutional Committee for the Ethical Review of Projects (CIREP) by phone (+34935422186) or by email (secretaria.cirep@upf.edu). CIREP is not part of the research team and will treat any information you send confidentially.

Risks and benefits: You will be exposed to news on climate change which may potentially be a sensitive issue. We cannot and do not guarantee that you will receive any benefits from this study apart from the compensation stated below.

Compensation: Your participation will be compensated on completion of the experiment. The compensation is $15/hour, above the recommended prolific rate of $12/hour, +$5 bonus and a participation to the lottery if conditions are met.

Data protection: In order to protect your privacy, we will not identify your data with your Name or Prolific ID, but rather with a code that will only be known to the research team members. The data collected during the experiment cannot be used to identify you (except via the Prolific ID, but once data collection is over, we will destroy the file matching your experimental data and your Prolific ID. Thus, once the experiment concludes, we will not be able to delete the data you generated, should you ask for it.
In case of withdrawal during the experiment, your data will be deleted and none of it will be used for the experiment or for the eventual publication. In order to make data only accessible to research team members, physical data will be stored in a locked secure location and digital data will be stored with access control systems. In the event of data publication, only anonymous data will be published.
Anonymized data may be used for other research projects and hosted or published in a public repository.
Payment-related data will be kept for the duration of the project and for its scientific validation and according to UPF regulations.

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) 2016/679 (EU), we provide the following information:
Data controller: Universitat Pompeu Fabra. C. de la Mercè, 12. 08002 Barcelona. Tel. +34 93 542 20 00. You can contact UPF’s Data Protection officer by sending an email to dpd@upf.edu.
Purposes of the processing: Carrying out the above-mentioned research project. Personal data will be aggregated during the initial stages of processing and deleted during the execution of the project. If a payment is involved, the necessary data will be kept in accordance with applicable regulation.
Legal basis: Your consent, which can be withdrawn at any time.
Recipients: Your personal data will only be processed by Universitat Pompeu Fabra and will not be transferred to third parties without your consent, except as otherwise provided by law. Data may be anonymized and published in an open science repository.
Rights: The personal data collected during the experiment will be aggregated soon after the experiment so It won’t be able to be used to identify you. Thus, after a final consent agreement at the conclusion of the experiment, we will not be able to delete the data you generated, should you ask for it. You can contact UPF’s Data Protection officer (dpd@upf.edu) for any queries or if you feel that your rights are not properly respected. Should you not be satisfied, you may file a complaint with the Catalan Data Protection Authority (apdcat.gencat.cat).
