Thank you for participating. You will be asked a few demographic questions and a short survey on your opinion on climate change, after this you will access a trial round of the experiment. In this round, you will interact with a simulated news feed.

Your Instructions:

  • Review the Feed: You will see a list of news headlines.
  • Read More (Optional): You can click "Read Full Story" to view the summary of an article before making your decision.
  • Make a Decision: For each article, decide whether you will have to chose either "Share" or "Do not Share". Your neighbors in the network will receive the articles you share.
